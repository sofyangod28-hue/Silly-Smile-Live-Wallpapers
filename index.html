Silly Smile Live Wallpapers
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Silly Smile Live Wallpapers is a wallpaper application published by READY COM. This policy explains how the app and its advertising services handle information.

1. App features

Wallpapers are bundled with the app and can be browsed offline. No account is required. Setting or sharing a wallpaper creates a local copy on your device. A selected live wallpaper is kept in app-specific storage so that it can continue playing. The app does not upload your wallpaper files to a developer-operated server. If you share an image, the app or service you select handles it under its own policy.

The app does not request your contacts, camera, microphone, phone number, or precise GPS location. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them to respond.

2. Advertising

The app uses Unity LevelPlay to request banner advertisements in wallpaper lists and an interstitial after every third wallpaper opening, when an ad is ready. Advertisements require an internet connection.

The Android build includes Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, and Google AdMob advertising SDKs or adapters. Which networks serve advertisements depends on the LevelPlay dashboard configuration and ad availability. Including an adapter does not mean that its network serves every user.

Advertising services may process advertising or other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and operating system information, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. They use this information to deliver and measure advertising, operate their services, and detect fraud. Their collection, sharing, retention, and privacy controls depend on their policies and the settings applied.

3. Your advertising choice

When the app starts, it asks whether you agree to personalized ad processing. Your choice is passed to LevelPlay as consent values for Unity and Facebook before initialization. The current app does not save this answer between app launches, so the question can appear again.

The current app does not use Google UMP or explicitly pass this choice to Google through a Google-specific consent API. This dialog is not a guarantee that every included network has received or applied the choice.

Choosing “No thanks” does not disable all advertising or all data processing. You can manage available advertising ID and ad privacy controls in Android settings and use the providers’ privacy controls. You can contact us about your choice or privacy concerns.

4. Advertising provider policies

  • Unity / LevelPlay / ironSource Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Permissions and local storage

The app uses internet access for advertising, Android advertising ID access for advertising services, and permission to set your wallpaper.

Temporary images and selected live wallpaper copies are kept in app-specific storage. Android may remove temporary files. You can clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove its local data. These actions do not necessarily remove a static wallpaper already applied to the device or copies you shared elsewhere.

6. Retention and international processing

Temporary images remain until removed by Android or by clearing the app’s local data. Selected live wallpaper copies remain in app-specific storage until the app’s data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

We retain support emails as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations. Advertising providers determine retention of the information they process under their own policies; we do not set a single retention period for them. Their processing may take place in countries other than your own.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information, or withdraw consent.

Email kolonil97@outlook.com for requests concerning information held by READY COM. Requests concerning information held by an advertising provider may need to be submitted through that provider’s privacy tools. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact kolonil97@outlook.com so we can review the request.

9. Security and changes

No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. We may revise this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date identifies this revision.
